Abstract

TL;DR ACE corrects Marginal Path Collapse when combining pretrained generative models.

Say what the paper studies and what it finds. The examples below use figures and results from the ACE project.

Method

Synthetic Checker

Two coordinates \(X,Y\) are generated from heterogeneous marginal and joint experts.

\(p(X,Y\mid A,B) \propto \dfrac{p(X\mid A)\,p(X,Y\mid B)}{p(X)}\)

Flexible-Pose Scaffold Decoration

Topology acts locally on the scaffold, while binding acts globally.

\(\begin{aligned} p(M^{sc},M^R\mid &A,B)\\ &\propto \dfrac{p(M^{sc}\mid A)\,p(M\mid B)}{p(M^{sc})} \end{aligned}\)

Multi-Instance Image Generation

Region prompts constrain local foregrounds, while a global prompt constrains the full image.
